# Pizza Dough

*Two batch sizes depending on how many mouths you're feeding.*

---

## Small Batch (2 Medium Pizzas)

### Ingredients
- 2½ cups bread flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup warm water (105°F to 115°F)
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2¼ teaspoons instant yeast (Fleischmann's or SAF)

---

## Large Batch (3-4 Medium or 2 Large Pizzas)

### Ingredients
- 5 cups bread flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons olive oil (+ 1 tablespoon for coating bowl)
- 1 cup warm water (105°F to 115°F)
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 4½ teaspoons instant yeast

---

## Method

1. **Bloom the yeast** — Mix warm water (105-115°F) with sugar, then add yeast. Let sit 10 minutes until yeast rises and gets foamy.

2. **Combine** — Add olive oil to flour and mix slightly. Add yeast mixture to flour in stand mixer.

3. **Knead** — Mix slowly 4-8 minutes until ball is smooth and slightly sticky. Don't overwork it.

4. **First rise** — Rest dough in olive oil-coated bowl, covered with moist cloth, for 45 minutes.

5. **Divide** — Split into dough balls and keep covered until ready to shape.

6. **Shape** — Use your hands (not a roller) to stretch pizza to desired thickness. Let the dough do the work.

---

## Notes
- Bread flour > all-purpose for better chew
- Water temp matters — too hot kills yeast, too cold won't activate it
- Hand-stretching gives you better texture than rolling
