# Bobby Hansen Jr.'s Quick Beurre Monté

*The fancy French trick that turns plain butter into a silky sauce that won't break.*

**Science bit:** Whisking cold butter into a tiny bit of hot water creates a stable emulsion (fat droplets suspended in water), so it stays smooth and glossy instead of separating into greasy oil and watery mess.

## Details
- **Makes:** Enough for 2 lbs veggies or 4 servings
- **Time:** ~10 minutes

## Ingredients
- 1 stick (½ cup / 4 oz) cold unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon water (or white wine/lemon juice/stock for extra flavor)
- Salt & pepper to taste (optional)

## Method
1. Cut the cold butter into tablespoon-sized chunks. Keep them in the fridge until right before using.
2. In a small saucepan, heat the 1 tablespoon water over low heat until it's steaming (not boiling—just hot with tiny bubbles at the edges if any).
3. Pull the pan off the heat. Add one butter chunk and whisk like crazy until it's almost melted and blended in.
4. Add the next chunk right away—keep whisking constantly. Repeat until all butter is in. If it starts bubbling around the edges, take it off the heat for a sec while whisking. It'll turn thick, smooth, and pale yellow.
5. Taste, add salt/pepper if you want. Use right away or keep warm on super-low heat (it stays saucy if not too hot).

## Quick Uses
- Toss with hot steamed veggies (asparagus, broccoli, green beans)
- Spoon over fish, steak, or lobster
- Drizzle on pasta or poach seafood in it

## Pro Move
Start with cold butter + constant whisking = no breaks. If it thickens too much later, whisk in a tiny splash of warm water to loosen it.
