Sourdough Bread

Yield: 1 loaf Total: ~20 hours Bake: 475°F
Ingredient Feed Starter Mix Dough Stretch & Fold Bulk Ferment Shape Final Rise Bake
Active starter~100g ripe ●Feed 12pm Mix in
Bread flour500g ●Add dry
Water350g, room temp ●Add & mix
Salt10g Add after autolyse
The dough ●3x, 30 min apart Overnight ~10h Pre-shape + final 1.5–2h room temp Lid on 25 min, off 25 min

Instructions

Day before — Feed your starter around noon so it peaks by late afternoon. At 5pm, mix the flour, water, and ripe starter until shaggy. Let rest 20–30 minutes (autolyse), then add the salt and knead it in. Over the next 1.5 hours, do three stretch-and-folds every 30 minutes — pull the dough up and fold it over itself, rotating the bowl each time. Cover and let bulk ferment at room temperature overnight, about 10 hours.

Bake day — At 6am, turn the dough onto a floured surface and pre-shape into a loose round. Rest 20–30 minutes, then shape tightly and place seam-side up in a floured banneton or bowl. Let it rise at room temperature for 1.5 to 2 hours. With 45 minutes to go, preheat your oven to 475°F with a Dutch oven inside. Turn the dough out onto parchment, score the top, and load into the hot Dutch oven. Bake covered for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake another 20–25 minutes until deep golden brown. Internal temp should hit 205–210°F. Cool at least 1 hour before slicing — the hardest part.
