Pizza Dough

Small: 2 medium pizzas Large: 3-4 medium or 2 large Time: ~1 hr 15 min
Ingredient Bloom Yeast Mix & Knead First Rise Shape
Bread flour2½ cups (small) / 5 cups (large) Add to mixer
Warm water1 cup, 105-115°F Mix with sugar & yeast Add to flour
Instant yeast2¼ tsp (small) / 4½ tsp (large) Bloom 10 min until foamy
Sugar1 tbsp Add to water
Olive oil2 tbsp (small) / 3 tbsp (large) + extra Add to flour Coat bowl
Salt¼ tsp (small) / 1 tsp (large) Add to flour
The dough Yeast blooms 10 min Knead 4-8 min, smooth & slightly sticky Rest 45 min, covered Hand-stretch, no roller

Instructions

Bloom the yeast by mixing warm water at 105-115°F with sugar, then add the yeast. Let it sit for 10 minutes until it rises and gets foamy. Add olive oil to the flour and mix slightly, then add the yeast mixture in a stand mixer. Knead slowly for 4-8 minutes until the ball is smooth and slightly sticky — don't overwork it. Rest the dough in an olive oil-coated bowl, covered with a moist cloth, for 45 minutes. Split into dough balls and keep covered until ready to shape. Use your hands, not a roller, to stretch the pizza to your desired thickness — let the dough do the work. Bread flour beats all-purpose for better chew. Water temperature matters: too hot kills the yeast, too cold won't activate it.
