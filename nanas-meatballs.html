Nana's Meatballs

Yield: ~40 meatballs Fry: 15–20 min Meat: 4 lbs beef
Ingredient Mix Dry Add Wet Roll Fry

Instructions

In a large bowl, combine the bread crumbs, black pepper, oregano, and Italian seasoning. Drizzle in the olive oil and mix until the crumbs are evenly coated — this keeps the meatballs tender. Add the ground beef, scrambled eggs, chopped onions, celery, garlic, parsley, Romano, and Parmesan. Use your hands to mix everything together gently; overworking the meat makes tough meatballs.

Roll the mixture into balls about the size of an "OK" sign circle — roughly 1½ inches across. You should get about 40 meatballs from this batch.

Pour olive oil into a heavy pan to about 2 inches deep and heat over medium-high. Fry the meatballs in batches, about ¾ submerged, turning occasionally, until deeply browned and cooked through — roughly 15 to 20 minutes. Drain on paper towels. Serve with Sunday Sauce over pasta, on sandwiches, or however Nana would do it.
