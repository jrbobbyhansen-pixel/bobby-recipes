# Roux and Gumbo

*Louisiana-style gumbo with a proper dark roux. This takes time but it's worth every minute.*

## Ingredients

**For the Roux:**
- About a cup of flour
- Half a cup of bacon grease
- Half a stick of butter

**Trinity + Aromatics:**
- A cup of chopped celery
- A whole sweet onion
- 1 green bell pepper
- Half a red bell pepper
- Four cloves of garlic

**Proteins:**
- About a pound of andouille sausage
- Shrimp
- Chicken thighs
- Okra and lump crab (optional but good)

**The Pot:**
- 2-3 quarts of beef and chicken stock (I mix them)
- Half a spoon of sugar
- Tabasco/Louisiana hot sauce to taste
- Salt to taste
- Old Bay and Tony Chachere's mixed, to taste
- One can of stewed tomatoes
- Half a tablespoon of Italian seasoning
- 4 tablespoons of gumbo filé
- 5 or so bay leaves
- 2 tablespoons of Worcestershire sauce
- Save a little bacon grease for the end

---

## The Roux

Start your roux by first cracking a beer and making sure you've got more cold and ready to go, then start whisking the flour, butter, and bacon drippings together in a saucepan on medium-low. 

**Don't stop stirring!!** 

Cook down the roux until it turns dark brown... you'll think it's starting to smell burned but it's not. Keep going a little longer til it's close to a chocolate color and thickens up like peanut butter. This can take 30 minutes or so. Just watch your heat carefully. 

Take it off the burner and keep whisking until it's not cooking anymore.

---

## Building the Base

Mix your celery, onion, bell pepper, and garlic and either blend it up or dice it all together. Stir all that and your sliced sausage into the roux. Simmer over low heat and cook together until vegetables are soft — usually about 12-15 minutes.

Get another beer if you haven't already. Take this off the burner and set it aside for a minute.

---

## The Gumbo Pot

Mix about equal water and beef/chicken stock in your gumbo pot and bring to a boil. Then slowly mix in the roux into the boiling water. 

Keep the heat to a simmer, and mix in the sugar, hot sauce, seasoning, bay leaves, stewed tomatoes, and maybe a little tomato sauce. 

Simmer this and relax for about an hour, then add your filé.

---

## Finishing

Put your bacon grease and maybe a little butter in a pan and cook the chicken, shrimp, crab, okra with a little vinegar (optional) for 15 minutes or so.

Take out the okra first and stir into the gumbo. Then mix in crabmeat, shrimp, chicken, and Worcestershire sauce, and simmer about 45 more minutes.

Just before serving, stir in a couple more teaspoons of filé gumbo powder.
