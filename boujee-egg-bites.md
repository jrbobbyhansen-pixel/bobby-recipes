# Bobby's Boujee Egg Bites

*Sous vide egg bites that put Starbucks to shame. Creamy, cheesy, with a Texas kick.*

## Details
- **Makes:** 10 egg bites
- **Time:** ~1 hour 20 minutes (mostly hands-off)
- **Equipment:** Sous vide circulator, 4 oz mason jars

## Ingredients

**Base:**
- 10 eggs
- 16 oz small curd cottage cheese
- 8 oz Swiss & Gruyère cheese (shredded)
- Bacon bits
- Salt & pepper

**Toppings:**
- HEB Sweet Texas Heat Seasoning
- Avocado, sliced
- Yellowbird Agave Sriracha

## Method

1. **Blend** eggs, cottage cheese, salt & pepper until creamy and smooth.

2. **Pour** the mixture into 10 greased 4 oz mason jars (leave a little room at the top).

3. **Stuff** each jar with shredded cheese and bacon bits — push them down into the egg mixture.

4. **Cook** in a sous vide bath for 1 hour 10 minutes at 167°F.

5. **Top** with sliced avocado, a drizzle of agave sriracha, and a sprinkle of Sweet Texas Heat Seasoning.

**Enjoy.** 👨‍🍳

---

## Notes
- The cottage cheese is the secret — gives them that silky, custard-like texture
- Can prep jars the night before and cook in the morning
- Lids finger-tight only (not sealed) so pressure can release
