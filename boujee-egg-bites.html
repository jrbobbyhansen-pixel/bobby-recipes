Boujee Egg Bites

Makes: 10 egg bites Active: ~10 min Total: ~1 hr 20 min
Ingredient Blend Fill Jars Sous Vide Top & Serve

Cottage cheese16 oz, small curd Blend until creamy

Instructions

Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, salt, and pepper until creamy and smooth. Pour the mixture into 10 greased 4-ounce mason jars, leaving a little room at the top. Stuff each jar with shredded Swiss and Gruyère cheese and bacon bits, pushing them down into the egg mixture. Cook in a sous vide bath for 1 hour 10 minutes at 167°F — lids should be finger-tight only, not sealed, so pressure can release. Top each bite with sliced avocado, a drizzle of Yellowbird Agave Sriracha, and a sprinkle of HEB Sweet Texas Heat Seasoning. The cottage cheese is the secret — it gives them that silky, custard-like texture that puts Starbucks to shame. You can prep the jars the night before and cook in the morning.
