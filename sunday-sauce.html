Sunday Sauce

Yield: ~1½ gallons Simmer: 2–3 hours Heat: Low & slow
Ingredient Prep Sauté Simmer
Tomato sauce106 oz (3 cans) Open cans
Peeled tomatoes102 oz (3 cans) Crush by hand
Onion1 large, diced Sauté until soft
Garlic4 cloves, minced Add last minute
Olive oil3 tbsp Heat for sauté
Fresh basilHandful, torn Add to pot
Fennel seed1 tsp Add to pot
Dried oregano1 tbsp Add to pot
Salt & pepperTo taste Season
The whole pot Low simmer, 2–3 hours

Instructions

Open the cans of tomato sauce and set aside. Open the peeled tomatoes and crush them by hand in a bowl — you want some chunks, not a puree. Heat the olive oil in a large, heavy pot over medium heat. Sauté the diced onion until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes, then add the minced garlic and cook just until fragrant, about 30 seconds more.

Pour in the tomato sauce and the hand-crushed tomatoes. Stir in the torn basil, fennel seed, oregano, and a generous pinch of salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Let it bubble lazily for 2 to 3 hours, stirring occasionally. The sauce should thicken and deepen in color. Taste and adjust salt before serving. Perfect over pasta, with Nana's Meatballs, or ladled onto a Sunday gravy plate.
