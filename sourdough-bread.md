# Sourdough Bread — Morning Bake Schedule

*Target: Fresh bread out of the oven by 8:00-8:30 AM*

## Overview
- Final proof: 2 hours
- Bulk fermentation: 10 hours (overnight)
- Stretch & fold window: 3 hours
- Starter peaks ~5 hours after feeding

---

## The Schedule

### Day Before

**12:00 PM** — Feed your starter so it peaks late afternoon

**5:00 PM** — Mix dough with ripe starter (flour, water, salt, starter). Optional 20-30 min rest (autolyse)

**5:30 PM** — Stretch & fold #1

**6:00 PM** — Stretch & fold #2

**6:30 PM** — Stretch & fold #3 (optional extra at 7:00 PM if dough feels weak)

**8:00 PM** — Start long bulk fermentation. Cover and leave at room temp until morning.

---

### Bake Day

**6:00 AM** — Turn dough out and pre-shape into loose round. Rest 20-30 minutes.

**6:30 AM** — Final shape and place in floured banneton or bowl.

**6:30-8:00 AM** — Final rise at room temperature (about 1.5-2 hours)

**7:15 AM** — Preheat oven AND Dutch oven to 475-500°F

**8:00 AM** — Turn dough out, score, load into hot Dutch oven, and bake.

---

---

## Alternate Schedule — 8:30 AM Bake

*Shift everything 30 minutes later if you want bread at 8:30 instead of 8:00*

### Day Before
- **12:30 PM** — Feed starter
- **5:30 PM** — Mix dough
- **6:00-7:00 PM** — Stretch & folds (6:00, 6:30, 7:00 PM)
- **8:30 PM** — Start long bulk (8:30 PM → 6:30 AM)

### Bake Day
- **6:30 AM** — Pre-shape; rest 20-30 minutes
- **7:00 AM** — Final shape and into basket
- **7:00-8:30 AM** — Final rise at room temperature
- **7:45 AM** — Preheat oven and Dutch oven
- **8:30 AM** — Bake

---

## Baking Notes
- Dutch oven lid on for first 20-25 minutes (creates steam for crust)
- Remove lid and bake another 20-25 minutes until deep golden brown
- Internal temp should hit 205-210°F
- Let cool at least 1 hour before cutting (the hardest part)
