# Balsamic Strawberries over HEB 1905 Vanilla

*Dead simple, fancy as hell. 5 minutes active, looks like a $15 restaurant dessert.*

## Details
- **Serves:** 4
- **Time:** 5 min active + 15-30 min macerating
- **Difficulty:** Stupid easy

## Ingredients
- 1 lb fresh strawberries, hulled & quartered
- 2 tbsp good balsamic vinegar
- ¼ cup sugar (less if berries are already sweet)
- ¼ tsp black pepper (optional, but do it)
- Fresh mint or basil (optional garnish)
- HEB Creamy Creations 1905 Vanilla Ice Cream

## Method

1. **Toss** strawberries with balsamic, sugar, and black pepper in a bowl.

2. **Let macerate** at room temp for 15-30 minutes. The berries will release their juices and create a syrupy sauce. This is the magic step — don't skip it.

3. **Scoop** the 1905 Vanilla into bowls. Spoon the berries and all that syrup over the top.

4. **Garnish** with torn mint or basil if you're feeling fancy.

## Notes
- Use ripe, in-season strawberries — makes all the difference
- The black pepper sounds weird but adds a subtle kick that works
- Can prep the berries ahead and refrigerate up to a few hours
