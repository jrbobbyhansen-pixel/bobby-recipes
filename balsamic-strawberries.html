Balsamic Strawberries

Serves: 2-4 Time: ~15 min
Ingredient Prep Berries Make Syrup Finish
Strawberries1 lb, hulled & halved Hull & halve
Balsamic vinegar2 tbsp Reduce with sugar
Sugar1 tbsp Dissolve in vinegar
Fresh minta few leaves, chiffonade Toss in
Black pepperfresh cracked Finish with pepper
The bowl Berries halved Simmer balsamic + sugar 2-3 min Toss, rest 5 min, serve

Instructions

Hull and halve the strawberries. In a small saucepan, simmer the balsamic vinegar and sugar over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until it thickens slightly into a light syrup. Toss the warm syrup with the strawberries, fresh mint chiffonade, and a generous crack of black pepper. Let it rest for 5 minutes so the berries macerate and the flavors meld. Serve over vanilla ice cream, Greek yogurt, pound cake, or just eat it with a spoon. The pepper is not optional — it's the secret that makes the balsamic and berries sing together.
