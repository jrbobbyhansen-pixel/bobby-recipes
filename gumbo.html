Roux and Gumbo

Serves: 8-10 Time: ~3-4 hours
Ingredient Make Roux Build Base Simmer Gumbo Cook Proteins Finish
Flour~1 cup Whisk with fat
Bacon grease1/2 cup + save some Whisk in Stir in at end
Butter1/2 stick Whisk in
Celery, onion, bell pepper1 cup each, chopped Sauté 12-15 min
Garlic4 cloves Add with trinity
Andouille sausage~1 lb, sliced Stir in
Beef & chicken stock2-3 qt, mixed Bring to boil, add roux
Stewed tomatoes1 can Add
SeasoningsOld Bay, Tony's, bay leaves, Italian, sugar, hot sauce, Worcestershire, filé Add all, simmer 1 hr Add more filé before serving
Chicken thighs~1 lb Pan cook 15 min Stir into gumbo
Shrimp~1 lb Pan cook Stir in
Okra & lump craboptional Cook with vinegar Stir in okra first, then crab
The pot Dark brown roux, ~30 min Veg soft, sausage in Simmer 1 hr, add filé Cook proteins separate Simmer 45 min more, serve

Instructions

Start your roux by cracking a beer and making sure you've got more cold and ready to go. Whisk the flour, butter, and bacon drippings together in a saucepan on medium-low. Don't stop stirring. Cook until it turns dark brown — you'll think it smells burned but it's not. Keep going until it's close to a chocolate color and thickens like peanut butter, about 30 minutes. Take it off the burner and keep whisking until it stops cooking. Mix your celery, onion, bell pepper, and garlic (blend or dice), then stir them and the sliced sausage into the roux. Simmer over low heat until vegetables are soft, about 12-15 minutes. Get another beer. Mix equal water and beef/chicken stock in your gumbo pot and bring to a boil, then slowly mix in the roux. Keep at a simmer and add sugar, hot sauce, seasonings, bay leaves, stewed tomatoes, and a little tomato sauce. Simmer for about an hour, then add your filé. In a separate pan, cook the chicken, shrimp, crab, and okra with a little bacon grease and butter for about 15 minutes. Stir the okra into the gumbo first, then the crabmeat, shrimp, chicken, and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer about 45 more minutes. Just before serving, stir in a couple more teaspoons of filé gumbo powder.
