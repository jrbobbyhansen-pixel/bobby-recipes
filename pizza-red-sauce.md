# Pizza Red Sauce

*Not from a jar. Takes 15 minutes and makes all the difference.*

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced (about 2 tsp)
- 2 (6 oz) cans tomato paste
- 1½ cups water
- 3 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried basil
- 2 tbsp parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp anchovy paste (trust me)
- 1 tsp red hot sauce (or ½ tsp cayenne)
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 tbsp red wine (optional but do it)

## Method

1. **Sauté** garlic in olive oil over medium heat until fragrant (1-2 minutes, don't burn it).

2. **Add** tomato paste, water, all dry ingredients, anchovy paste, parmesan, and wine.

3. **Simmer** over medium-low heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.

4. **Adjust** salt, pepper, and hot sauce to taste.

## Notes
- The anchovy paste sounds weird but adds umami depth without tasting fishy
- Makes enough for 3-4 pizzas
- Keeps in fridge for a week, freezes well
